About About this study

This app is developed by Imran S. A. Khan, a PhD student at Le Mans University, to make learning accessible through short, bite-sized experiences.

This particular app focuses on environmental and climate education, helping you explore important topics that may fall outside your usual field of study.

Why are you here?

Before making the app available to the wider public, we want to understand: Whether bite-sized learning can effectively improve learning.

Your participation will help us evaluate and improve this initiative.
